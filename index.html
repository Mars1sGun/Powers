⚡ What is TempU?

TempU is a special temporary power-up hero that boosts your abilities for a short duration. While TempU is active:

  • Power ability cooldowns are reduced (see table below)
  • Potion effects are amplified by +1 level
  • Lazer beam is enabled (requires holding Lazer item + Butcher hero)
  • Tentacle range and max hits are increased
